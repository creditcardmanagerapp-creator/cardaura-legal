Buses Untangled Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Summary

Buses Untangled has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad service needs to show ads.

What is stored on your device

Your level progress, which levels you have cleared, which tips you have seen, and settings such as sound. This data lives in the app's private storage on your device.

Advertising

Buses Untangled uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising if you allow tracking. You are asked for permission through Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, a consent form. You can change your choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or with "Privacy options" in the app's settings when it is shown. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and sharing

Buses Untangled does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and does not sell your data.

Your control

Delete the app to remove all its data from your device.

Children

Buses Untangled is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
