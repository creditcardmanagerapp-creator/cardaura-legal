CardAura Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Summary

Your card and spend data stays on your device. CardAura has no accounts and no servers that receive your financial data. The only data that leaves your device is what the ad service and Apple need to show ads and process purchases.

What is stored on your device

The cards you add and their settings, spend records you enter or import, budgets, fee-waiver and milestone progress, and preferences. For SMS imports only the card's last 4 digits, amount, merchant, date and category are kept, not the full message or full card numbers. This data lives in the app's private storage and in an app group shared with CardAura's own share extension and Shortcuts.

SMS and screenshots

Messages are read by an Apple Shortcut that you run and then handed to CardAura on your device. Screenshots you share are read on your device. None of this is uploaded by CardAura.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan UPI QR codes on your device.

Advertising

CardAura uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising if you allow tracking. On iOS you are asked for permission through Apple's tracking prompt, and where required a consent form. You can change your choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or via "Privacy choices" in the Go ad-free screen. Subscribing removes ads. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. CardAura receives only whether your subscription is active, not your payment details.

Analytics and sharing

CardAura does not use analytics or crash-reporting services and does not sell your data.

Your control

Delete a card or spend record in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove all its data from your device. Backups you keep through iCloud or your computer are governed by Apple's settings.

Children

CardAura is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes materially you will be asked to review it again. Last updated 31 October 2026.
