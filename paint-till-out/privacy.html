Paint Till Out Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Summary

Paint Till Out has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad service needs to show ads, and what you share with nearby players in a local multiplayer game.

What is stored on your device

Your player name, coins, XP and rank, gear and upgrades, squad and recruits, championship progress, achievements, training scores, career stats and settings such as controls, graphics and sound. Maps you build in the editor and mod packs are saved as files in the app's Documents folder, which you can see in the Files app. All of this stays on your device.

Motion sensors and controllers

If you turn on gyro aim, the app reads your device's motion sensor to aim. Game controller, keyboard and mouse input is used only to play. This data is used on your device during play and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Local multiplayer

When you host or join a game, Paint Till Out uses Bonjour over your local Wi-Fi to find nearby players. Your player name, team, loadout, your position and actions during the match, and any custom map the host plays are sent only to the other devices in that game. Nothing is sent to a server of ours, and nothing is kept after the game ends. iOS asks your permission before the app uses the local network.

Advertising

Paint Till Out uses Google AdMob to show ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch for double coins, and an occasional full-screen ad between matches. Ads never appear during a match. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising if you allow tracking. You are asked for permission through Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, a consent form. You can change your choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or with "Ad privacy options" in the app's settings when it is shown. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and sharing

Paint Till Out does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and does not sell your data.

Your control

Delete the app to remove all its data from your device.

Children

Paint Till Out is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
