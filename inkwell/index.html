Inkwell

A private journal for iPhone and iPad. Write entries, add photos, voice notes and scanned pages, track moods and habits, and look back with memories, insights and time capsules. Your journal stays on your device, with optional iCloud sync and encrypted backups.

Support

For help, bug reports or feedback, email creditcardmanagerapp@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version, but never send your journal content or passcode.

Common questions

I forgot my Inkwell passcode. You can unlock with Face ID or your device passcode. The Inkwell passcode and vault passcodes can't be recovered by us; we never see them.

How do I move my journal to a new device? Turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, or make an encrypted backup in Settings > Backup & Restore and restore it on the new device.

Is Inkwell free? Yes. Every feature is free, with no purchases or subscriptions. A small banner ad appears at the bottom of Insights and Memories, never while you write.
