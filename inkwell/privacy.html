Inkwell Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Summary

Inkwell is a private journal. There are no Inkwell accounts and no servers of ours, and we can't read your journal. Everything you write and add stays on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync or export a backup. The only data that leaves your device without you choosing it is what Google's ad service needs to show a small ad (never while you write).

Your journal

Entries, moods, tags, trackers, prompts you answer, birthdays, time capsules and vaults are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Photos, videos, voice notes and files you attach are encrypted on your device before they are stored. You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID, your device passcode or an Inkwell passcode; Face ID data is handled by iOS and never reaches the app.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud, your journal is stored in your own private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit, so it can sync between your devices. It is protected by your Apple Account and Apple's iCloud security, and we have no access to it. Turn sync off at any time in Settings.

Encrypted backups (optional)

Backups you export are encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you decide. We never receive them and can't recover a lost backup password.

On-device intelligence

Summaries, insights, writing prompts, mood suggestions, text recognition in scanned pages and voice-note transcription all run on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks (including Apple Intelligence where available). Your writing is not sent to any AI service.

Permissions

Inkwell asks before using: the camera (photos, videos and scanned pages), the microphone and speech recognition (voice notes and on-device transcription), your location (only when you tag an entry; the place name is looked up through Apple Maps, and the location is saved only in your journal), contacts (only if you import birthdays; just names and birthdays are saved), photos you pick, and notifications (reminders, which never show what you wrote). You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

Widgets

Inkwell widgets show your streak, mood and "on this day" counts. They receive a small summary without any of your journal text.

Advertising

The free version shows a small banner ad outside the editor, lock screen and vaults, served by Google AdMob. Inkwell requests non-personalised ads and does not ask to track you. Google may still collect your device's IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information and ad interaction data to serve, measure and protect ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice with "Privacy options" in the app. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and sharing

Inkwell has no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own, and we never sell or share your data.

Your control

Delete entries in the app, turn off iCloud sync, or delete the app to remove its data from your device. To delete synced data, also remove Inkwell's data in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Inkwell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
