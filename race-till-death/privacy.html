Race till Death Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Summary

Race till Death has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's ad service needs to show ads, and what you share with nearby players in a local race.

What is stored on your device

Your coins, cars, upgrades, paint and liveries, race stats, best laps and ghost laps, GP season progress and settings such as sound, controls and graphics. This data lives in the app's private storage on your device.

Motion sensors and controllers

If you choose tilt steering, the app reads your device's motion sensor to steer. Game controller input is used only to drive. This data is used on your device during play and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Local multiplayer

When you host or join a room, Race till Death uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth to find nearby players. Your player name (your device name unless you change it), your car and your car's position and inputs are sent only to the other devices in that race. Nothing is sent to a server of ours, and nothing is kept after the race ends. iOS asks your permission before the app uses the local network.

Advertising

Race till Death uses Google AdMob to show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use it for personalised advertising if you allow tracking. You are asked for permission through Apple's tracking prompt and, where required, a consent form. You can change your choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or with "Privacy options" in the app's settings when it is shown. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytics and sharing

Race till Death does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own and does not sell your data.

Your control

Delete the app to remove all its data from your device.

Children

Race till Death is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
